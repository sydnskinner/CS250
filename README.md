- How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

User stories are incredibly helpful when creating a program because they are the initial essence, understanding and expectations laid out by the user and client. With the information provided in user stories we can translate and implement their needs into the code for the program. Because we have the outline of what they are expecting to receive when the project is completed, the team has a working baseline for expectations throughout the course of the projects development. Having open communication and dialogue, while also getting updates to user stories along the way, will enable us to deliver the p[erfect project to the user at the end of the peojects completion. 

- How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

Scrum events like daily meetings and sprints will be essential in developing programs. Using these tools and ensuring that the entire team can have an open communication with one another will enable us to understand where every team member is at any given point. Holding daily meetings and having a review after every sprint will allow us to see where our faults and strengths lie, so that we can improve moving into the following weeks of the projects production. Making use of a whiteboard with all of the tasks that are in progress, completed and yet to be started will also help immensely with understanding where we may need to pivot given the timeframe and deadline of the project.

- What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

There are many factors to being a good team member. Ensuring you are on time and not missing any meetings, being available during those meetings and speaking up to discuss and collaborate with fellow team members is the most important part. Keeping the sprint board updated, knowing where you end each day, and checking work before finalizing also go a long way to being a good team member. I think the largest part would be communication with other team members, because as much as there are individuals working on certain aspects, at the end of the day the entire team is working towards one goal in the end, so having everyone on board and transparent with one another will do the most good for the entire group and the project.
